1. Sent Mail

Sent emails are automatically stored in the "Sent" folder after successful delivery.

1 Navigate to Sent

Click the Sent folder in the sidebar. All successfully sent emails are listed here.

Screenshot:

2 View Message Details

Click on any sent message to view its full details, including recipients, subject, send time, and any delivery status.

3 Search Sent Mail

Use the search bar within the Sent folder to find emails by recipient, subject, or keyword.

4 Delete Sent Mail

Select sent messages and delete them. Note: this removes the copy from your view only; the copy on the IMAP server may persist depending on server settings.

5 Archived Sent Mail

Sent emails may be moved to archive based on your account settings. Access archived mail via the "Archive" folder.

2. Important Notes

  • Sent mail is the source of truth for email delivery records — the IMAP server is authoritative.
  • Deleted sent mail can often be recovered from the Trash within the email provider's retention window.
  • Archive policies vary by organization — some automatically archive sent mail after 90 days.
  • For BYOK setups, the IMAP server handles sent mail storage; TASMail proxies the connection.
