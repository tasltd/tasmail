1. Compose Email

To compose a new email in TASMail:

1 Open Composer

Click the Compose button (usually a "+" or "New Message" icon) in the top-right or sidebar.

2 Add Recipients

Enter recipient email addresses in the To field. Use the address book picker for contacts.

3 Add Subject

Enter the email subject in the subject line field.

4 Write Body

Use the rich text editor to compose your email. You can format text, add links, and insert images.

5 Add Attachments

Click the attachment icon to upload files from your device. Supported types include images, PDFs, and documents.

6 Schedule Send (Optional)

Use the "Send later" option to schedule the email for delivery at a specific date and time.

7 Send

Click the Send button. The email is queued and sent via the backend SMTP proxy.

2. Additional Notes

  • The rich text editor (TipTap) supports bold, italics, links, and image insertion.
  • Scheduled emails can be managed from the Drafts or Scheduled folder.
  • Attachments are uploaded via the API and stored in the email service's attachment backend.
  • If the SMTP server is unavailable, the email is saved as a draft and retried later.
