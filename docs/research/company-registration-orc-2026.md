# Ghana Company Registration — ORC 2026 Research Notes

**Date:** 2026-05-28
**Purpose:** Raw research notes feeding `docs/COMPANY-REGISTRATION-RUNBOOK.md` (TMAIL-43).
**Scope:** Current (2026) procedure for incorporating a Company Limited by Shares in Ghana
under the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992).

---

## 1. Governing Law

- **Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992)** — primary statute, replaced the Companies Act 1963 (Act 179).
  - Recognises four company types: (a) Company Limited by Shares, (b) Company Limited by
    Guarantee, (c) Unlimited Company, (d) External Company.
  - Minimum stated capital for a 100 %-Ghanaian-owned Company Limited by Shares: **GHS 500**.
  - Name reservation valid for **2 months** after fee payment.
- **Income Tax Act, 2015 (Act 896)** — TIN issuance via GRA, mandatory for all directors,
  shareholders, and the company secretary before incorporation can complete.
- **Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2020 (Act 1044)** — Beneficial Ownership (BO) declaration is a
  mandatory section in the incorporation forms.

## 2. The Authority

The **Office of the Registrar of Companies (ORC)** took over the company-registration mandate
from the Registrar General's Department (RGD) on 1 August 2020 (corporate-affairs split). RGD
retains marriages, intellectual property, and administrator-general functions.

- Website: <https://orc.gov.gh/>
- e-Registrar online portal: linked from orc.gov.gh homepage (login + KYC)
- Physical HQ: ORC Head Office, off Bannerman Road, Adabraka, Accra
- Regional offices: Kumasi, Takoradi, Tamale, Ho, Sunyani, Koforidua, Wa, Bolgatanga
- Expedited path: **Prestige Services** — guarantees turnaround within **48 hours** for an
  additional fee (line item on the 2026 fee schedule).

## 3. 2026 Fee Schedule (effective 2 Feb 2026)

Authoritative source: <https://orc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/FINAL-FEES-AND-CHARGES-2026.pdf>

Key line items relevant to TASMail (Company Limited by Shares, 100 % Ghanaian-owned):

| Item | Fee (GHS) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Name search & reservation | 120 | Valid 2 months |
| Filing fee — Company Limited by Shares (incorporation) | 230 | Base fee, excluding stamp duty |
| Stamp duty on stated capital | 0.5 % of stated capital | Min GHS 500 stated capital → GHS 2.50 stamp duty floor |
| Certified true copy of Certificate of Incorporation | 50 | Per copy; banks usually require 1–2 |
| Certified true copy of Form 3 (Commencement of Business) | 50 | Per copy |
| Beneficial Ownership filing | 50 | Mandatory under Act 1044 |
| Prestige Service surcharge (48-hour turnaround) | 750 | Optional, applies to total filing |
| TIN issuance (GRA) | 0 | No charge, 24–48 hours |

**Indicative total for TASMail registration**: ~GHS 500–550 standard path; ~GHS 1,250–1,300
with Prestige Service. Excludes any legal advisory fees if engaged.

## 4. Required Inputs Before Filing

| Input | How obtained | Lead time |
|---|---|---|
| Proposed company name (3 options ranked) | Self-chosen; check uniqueness via ORC name-search portal | Immediate |
| TIN for each director, shareholder, and the company secretary | GRA office or eServices Ghana portal | 24–48 h, no charge |
| Ghana Card (national ID) for each director, shareholder, secretary | Personal | Pre-existing |
| Registered office address (physical and digital — GhanaPostGPS code) | Tenant/owner of the premises | Immediate if you control the address |
| Proposed objects of the company (business activities) | Drafted from PRD / business plan | Immediate |
| Stated capital and share split | Board decision | Immediate |
| Beneficial Ownership declaration (≥10 % ownership / control) | Self-declared | Immediate |
| Company secretary | Must be a person qualified under §211 of Act 992 (lawyer, chartered accountant, ICSA member, or 3 years' company-secretary experience) | Depends on appointee |
| Auditor (consent letter) | Must be a chartered accountant licensed by ICAG | Depends on appointee |

## 5. Forms (Companies Act 2019 schedule)

- **Form 3** — Application for Incorporation of a Company (Limited by Shares)
- **Form 4** — Notice of Commencement of Business
- **BO Form** — Beneficial Ownership Declaration
- All forms are bundled in the e-Registrar online flow; paper forms are available at ORC.

## 6. Online Process (e-Registrar Portal)

1. Create an applicant account on the e-Registrar portal (Ghana Card verification step).
2. Submit name-availability search; on approval, the name is reserved 2 months.
3. Upload incorporation forms (Form 3, Form 4, BO Form), constitution (optional —
   default model constitution under Act 992 applies if none supplied), consent letters
   from directors/secretary/auditor.
4. Pay fees online (card or MoMo) or generate a payment voucher for in-house bank.
5. ORC reviews; on approval, downloads available:
   - Certificate of Incorporation
   - Certified Form 3
   - Certified Form 4 (Commencement)
   - TIN for the company (issued by GRA via the integrated flow)

Standard processing: **5–10 working days**. With Prestige Service: **≤48 hours**.

## 7. In-Person Process

Functionally identical to the online process but with paper forms submitted at the ORC HQ
or a regional office, fees paid at the in-house GCB Bank branch, and physical pickup of
certificates. Used when the e-Registrar portal is down (intermittent in past months per
operator reports) or when the applicant prefers face-to-face KYC.

## 8. Output Artifacts (and downstream consumers)

| Artifact | Downstream consumer |
|---|---|
| Certificate of Incorporation | Bank account opening, Paystack/Mastercard MPGS KYC, NCA telecoms registration, DPC controller registration, government tender pre-qualification |
| Form 3 (certified) | Same — usually requested alongside Cert of Inc |
| Form 4 (Commencement of Business) | GRA tax-clearance certificate eligibility; required to invoice corporate customers |
| Company TIN | All GRA tax filings (VAT, NHIL, GETFund, PAYE, withholding tax), Paystack settlement onboarding |
| Beneficial Ownership Certificate | Bank account opening (AML), Paystack/Mastercard MPGS KYC, government contracts |

## 9. Common Pitfalls (operator-reported)

- **Name rejection** for similarity to existing entities — submit 3 candidates ranked.
- **Secretary qualification** — non-qualified secretary appointments get bounced at review;
  use ICSA member or licensed lawyer for the first filing.
- **Stamp duty miscalculation** — must be 0.5 % of stated capital; understating triggers a
  query and resets the clock.
- **GhanaPostGPS code missing** — required for the registered office; obtain via the
  GhanaPostGPS app before filing.
- **Beneficial Ownership** — every individual with ≥10 % ownership or effective control must
  be declared; omitting an indirect shareholder triggers Act 1044 penalties later.
