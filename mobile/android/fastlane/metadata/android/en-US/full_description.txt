TASMail is a Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) email client built for Ghana and the rest of West Africa. Connect your existing Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, Zoho, FastMail, iCloud, or any IMAP mailbox — TASMail never stores your messages on our servers, it just gives you a fast, beautiful interface to read and send them.

WHY TASMAIL
• Works with the email account you already have — no migration needed
• End-to-end transport security: IMAPS + SMTPS, AES-256 credential storage
• Offline inbox that syncs when you're back online — built for low-bandwidth networks
• Biometric unlock (fingerprint / face)
• Snooze, scheduled send, templates, signatures, contacts, calendar invitations
• Native sharing — accept "Share via email" from any other app
• Local notifications (no Firebase tracking required)
• Localized for Twi, Ewe, Ga, Hausa, and English

PRIVACY FIRST
We do not read, scan, or sell your email. Your IMAP credentials are encrypted on-device with the OS keystore and on the server with AES-256-GCM. We do not run ad tracking, we do not require Google Play Services for core functionality, and Huawei devices without Google services are fully supported.
